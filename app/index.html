WELCOME TO YOUR BENCH

Build something.
Understand everything.

Start with PCB1, then follow the guide through power delivery, the processor, memory, thermal interface, cooling, the backplate and the bracket.

  1. Select a part. Read its role and reference identifier in the guide.
  2. Place it. Use the placement button or drag it onto its suggested outline.
  3. Make the connection. Answer a knowledge check, inspect installed parts, or view the GPU’s internal blocks.

Explore mode lets you move and rotate parts. Removing a part also removes components that depend on it; Undo restores the change.

This is a learning workbench. Package geometry, power phases and ratings are illustrative. The checks do not replace electrical simulation or PCB/ASIC design verification.

Ctrl/Cmd + Z: undo · Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + Z: redo · Escape: clear selection. Export your project to move it between browsers.
